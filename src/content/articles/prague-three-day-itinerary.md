Prague rewards slow walking more than ticking off sights. The historic centre is compact enough that you can see the highlights on foot, and the public transport is cheap and reliable when your legs give up. This plan keeps each day to one side of the river, so you aren't crossing back and forth, and it puts the busiest places early in the morning, before tour groups arrive.

## Before you go

- **Currency:** Czech koruna (CZK). Cards are accepted almost everywhere. When a card machine or ATM asks, always choose to pay **in CZK**, not in euros. The "convert for you" option uses a worse rate.
- **Cash:** you rarely need it. If you do, use a bank ATM and avoid street exchange booths advertising "0% commission".
- **Public transport:** one ticket covers metro, trams and buses. Buy in the official PID Lítačka app or at machines, and validate paper tickets when you board. A 24-hour or 72-hour pass usually pays off from your third ride.
- **Water:** tap water is safe to drink. Bring a bottle.
- **Best time:** April–June and September–October. December has Christmas markets but large crowds.

## Day 1

**Theme: Old Town and the Jewish Quarter (all on foot)**

- **07:00 – Charles Bridge.** Go before 8am. By 10am it's shoulder-to-shoulder. Walk it end to end (about 10 minutes) and come back.
- **08:30 – Breakfast in the Old Town.** Step one or two streets away from the main squares and prices drop noticeably.
- **09:30 – Old Town Square.** See the Astronomical Clock and Týn Church. The clock's show runs on the hour; the crowd is bigger than the show, so watching once is enough.
- **11:00 – Jewish Quarter (Josefov).** A 5-minute walk north. The synagogues and the Old Jewish Cemetery share one combined ticket. Allow 2 hours. Note that it is **closed on Saturdays** and Jewish holidays.
- **14:00 – Late lunch, then rest.**
- **17:00 – Letná Park.** Cross the river (15 minutes' walk) for the best free view over the bridges. The beer garden is popular at sunset, and soft drinks are available.

## Day 2

**Theme: Castle District and Malá Strana (the "Lesser Town")**

- **08:30 – Take tram 22 up to Prague Castle** (stop: Pražský hrad) so you walk *down* the hill all day instead of up.
- **09:00 – Prague Castle complex.** The courtyards and grounds are free. St Vitus Cathedral's main interior and the other historic buildings need a ticket. The basic circuit is enough for most visitors. Allow 2–3 hours.
- **12:00 – Walk down through Nerudova street** into Malá Strana for lunch.
- **14:00 – Kampa Island and the Lennon Wall.** Both are free and 10 minutes apart.
- **16:00 – Petřín Hill.** Walk up through the orchards (30 minutes, steep in parts) or take the funicular, which uses a normal public transport ticket. The lookout tower at the top is paid.
- **Evening:** cross back over the river for dinner in the New Town, where prices are lower than in Malá Strana.

## Day 3

**Theme: Local Prague**

- **Morning – Vyšehrad.** A hilltop fortress with a church, a cemetery where many famous Czechs are buried, and river views. The grounds are free and it's far quieter than the castle. Take metro line C to Vyšehrad.
- **Midday – Walk along the river** to the **Dancing House** (20 minutes), then continue to the National Theatre.
- **Afternoon – Choose one:**
  - **Vinohrady:** leafy residential streets, cafés and Riegrovy Sady park, with a sunset view of the castle.
  - **Museums:** the National Museum on Wenceslas Square for a rainy day.
- **Before you leave:** if you're flying, leave **60–75 minutes** to get to the airport by public transport.

## Where to stay in Prague

| Area | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old Town / Malá Strana | Walking everywhere, first visit | Highest prices, noisy at night |
| New Town (Nové Město) | Central and better value | Some streets busy with bars |
| Vinohrady | Quiet, local feel, good food | 10–15 min by tram/metro to the centre |
| Karlín / Holešovice | Modern, good value, cafés | Less "old Prague" atmosphere |

**Our pick for a first visit on a budget:** Vinohrady or New Town, close to a metro station.

## Getting around

- **From the airport:** there's no train. Take a city bus to the metro (regular public transport ticket), or the Airport Express bus straight to the main train station (separate fare, bought on board).
- **Taxis:** use a ride-hailing app instead of hailing a taxi on the street.
- **In the centre:** walk. Most of this itinerary is within a 30-minute walk of Old Town Square.
